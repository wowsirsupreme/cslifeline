How CS Lifeline uses data

CS Lifeline helps Computer Science teachers see which ideas students find difficult, so they can help sooner. This page explains, in plain language, what is recorded and who can see it. Your school is responsible for how it uses CS Lifeline and can answer questions about it.

What is recorded when you practise or take an assessment

  • Your answers, whether they were correct, and the marks.
  • How long you spent on each question, how many times you changed your answer and whether you opened a hint.
  • Whether the page was hidden while you answered (for example, your phone locked). This is only context. It is never treated as cheating and is never used to predict anything about you.

CS Lifeline does not record other apps or websites you use, your location, your contacts or identifiers of your device.

Who can see it

  • You see your own results, explanations, progress and suggested revision.
  • Your teacher sees results for the classes they teach, which ideas the class finds difficult, and early-warning indicators. These indicators are estimates to help a teacher decide whom to talk to first. They are not grades or judgements of ability, and they are never shown to students.
  • School administrators manage accounts and classes. Their actions are recorded in an audit log that cannot be changed.
  • Teachers in other classes or schools cannot see your records. This is enforced by the database rules, not just hidden on screen.

Research

CS Lifeline is also used in an MSc research study on learning analytics in IGCSE Computer Science. Research data is exported only with a pseudonymous research ID (no name, email or typed answers), and by default only for learners whose consent the school has recorded. Research use requires approval from the school and an ethics committee, and, where required, consent from a parent or guardian. You can ask your teacher to withdraw your research consent at any time; this does not affect your use of CS Lifeline.

Other learning platforms

Some schools also use WOW Sir or DBWOW. Data from those platforms is used by CS Lifeline only when the school has authorised it, only for Computer Science learning activity, and only when your accounts have been linked by a teacher who has checked it is really you. Names are never used to match accounts automatically.

How long it is kept, and your rights

Learning records are kept for the period your school sets (two years by default) and can be deleted earlier on request. You, or your parent or guardian, can ask the school to see, correct or delete your data. Administrators can erase a learner's learning records from within CS Lifeline.

Demonstration mode

The public demonstration uses invented (synthetic) learners only. Nothing typed in the demonstration leaves your browser.
